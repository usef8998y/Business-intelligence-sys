# Product Scope — Retail BI MVP

## Target customer
Retail business with multiple branches.

## Problem
Managers cannot easily connect sales, product, branch, inventory and cost data to explain changes in gross profit.

## Demo journey
1. Import sample CSV datasets.
2. Validate and store the data.
3. Calculate gross profit and compare two periods.
4. Ask a supported business question.
5. Review evidence and a proposed recommendation.
6. Approve or reject the recommendation; record the review.

## In scope
CSV import, validation, relational data model, deterministic metrics, supported questions, evidence, reviewable recommendations.

## Out of scope
Live ERP/POS integrations, autonomous actions, custom model training, government/defense workflows, Horus integration.
